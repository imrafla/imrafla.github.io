SEO & Architecture Audit

Current Issues

fleetleasingaustralia.com.au — 8 key problems affecting findability, clarity, and conversions

5 Section-Level 404s Critical

Some top-level pages do not exist, but their child pages do. This is confusing for users and search engines, and it weakens page visibility.

  • /vehicles → 404 (child: /vehicles/popular-models)
  • /deals → 404 (child: /deals/limited-time)
  • /resources → 404 (children: faqs, blogs)
  • /products → 404 (children: fleet-finance, business-finance)
  • /services → 404 (child: /services/consultation)

Flat & Fragmented Hierarchy High

Key pages are spread out without a clear grouping. People and search engines have a harder time understanding how pages relate to each other.

Orphaned & Ambiguous Pages High

/explore and /explore-body are unclear. The names do not explain what users will get, so these pages should be renamed or merged into clearer sections.

Missing SEO Hub Pages Medium

Important topics are only covered in blog posts. You need clear main pages for core services and categories so customers can find them faster.

Blog Depth = 4 Levels High

Blog posts are buried in a deep path (/resources/blogs/{slug}). A flatter blog path usually makes posts easier to find, easier to link internally, and easier to keep visible in search.

No Defined Content Strategy Medium

You have blog content, but not a clear plan that guides readers from information to enquiry. Supporting pages are missing.

500+ Vehicle Pages Lack Faceted Structure Medium

500+ vehicle pages are in one flat set. Adding category pages (for example, utes and vans) will make browsing easier and improve search visibility.

Missing Trust & Conversion Pages Medium

Trust-building pages are missing (testimonials, case studies, industry pages, tools). These pages usually help users decide and convert.

Architecture Direction

Solutions

Site Architecture Rationalization - Summary

Why These Changes Are Happening

The old structure had several practical problems:

  • Top-level pages that did not exist (/vehicles, /services, /products, /deals)
  • Pages trying to do too many things at once (for example, /explore)
  • Unnecessary nesting (for example, /about/contact)
  • Marketing campaigns mixed into core site structure
  • No clear rules for filter URLs

This update is designed to:

  • Remove fake/empty hierarchy
  • Match URLs to real business categories
  • Keep browsing structure separate from filters
  • Keep campaigns separate from inventory structure
  • Set clear long-term rules

In short: this moves the website from folder-driven structure to user-intent structure.

Problems: Artificial Section Hubs

Some parent pages looked important but had no real content.

That led to:

  • Wasted search-engine attention
  • Confusing site structure
  • Weak internal-link signals

Problems: Mixed Intent Pages

Pages like /explore mixed brand browsing, body-type browsing, and filters in one place. This made the page purpose unclear.

Problems: Campaign Confusion

"Limited Time" was treated in different ways (folder, filter, and page). That blurred the line between structure and marketing.

Problems: Uncontrolled Filter States

Without clear filter rules, too many similar URLs can appear. That can create duplicates and make it harder for search engines to pick the right page.

SEO, Crawling, and Indexing: Crawl Waste

Search engines can spend time on low-value pages (empty hubs, extra folders, endless filters) instead of key pages.

SEO, Crawling, and Indexing: Duplicate Content Risk

Many filter combinations can produce near-identical pages, which spreads ranking strength too thin.

SEO, Crawling, and Indexing: Diluted Authority

When one page tries to cover too much, no topic becomes strong enough. Clear topic sections usually perform better.

SEO, Crawling, and Indexing: Structural Instability

If temporary campaigns are built into core URL structure, future updates become harder and riskier.

What Is Improving

1. Clear Main Structure

  • /inventory/{brand}/{model}
  • /inventory/{brand}
  • /inventory/{body-slug}
  • /brands and /body-types as directory pages only
  • Dedicated finance sections
  • Campaign page (/deals)

Each page now has one clear purpose.

2. Clear Filter Rules

  • Filters are UX-only
  • Filtered states are noindex
  • Canonical back to base pages
  • No parameter URLs in sitemap
  • No multi-primary combinations

This keeps search results cleaner and avoids duplicate pages.

3. Stronger Topic Grouping

  • Brand authority
  • Body-type authority
  • Finance product clusters
  • Campaign acquisition surface

4. Clear Campaign Separation

/deals becomes a dedicated campaign page, while filter options stay as browsing tools.

Improved User Experience

1. Reduced Cognitive Load

Users can choose a clear path: Inventory, Brand Directory, Body Type Directory, Finance, or Limited-Time.

2. Clear Context Locking

From /brands, users route to /inventory/toyota. From /body-types, users route to /inventory/suv. This keeps context clear without extra SEO subpaths.

3. Campaign Clarity

Limited-time offers are easy to find, without disrupting normal browsing.

4. Logical Vehicle Detail Structure

Each model has one main page: /inventory/{brand}/{model}. Variants can live on that same page, which avoids fragmentation.

Future Proofing

1. Keep Attributes Out of Core URLs

Use cases (for example, Passenger vs Commercial) stay as filters, not folders. This avoids duplication.

2. Flexible Growth

You can add new brands, new body types, new finance products, and new campaigns without restructuring core URLs.

3. No Forced Migrations

Because the structure is cleaner, future redesigns should need fewer redirects and less rework.

4. Scalable SEO Strategy

This setup supports steady growth in content, categories, and campaigns without breaking structure.

Final Strategic Outcome

The site moves from a folder-driven structure to a clearer, intent-led structure that is easier to grow.

The result is:

  • Better crawl efficiency
  • Cleaner indexation
  • Stronger topical authority
  • Clearer user journeys
  • Reduced long-term structural risk
  • Improved marketing flexibility
